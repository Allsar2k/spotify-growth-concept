# 01 · Problem and opportunity

## The moment

Every year, millions of students graduate. For Spotify Premium Student subscribers, graduation is also a price change: once they can no longer verify as students, Spotify's terms move them to the full-price Premium plan (Spotify, n.d.), which costs roughly twice as much. It happens in the same months they move cities, start a first job and see their budget change completely.

**That is the most predictable churn moment Spotify has.** It is dated, it affects a whole cohort at once, and the people involved have often used Spotify every day for four years.

## Why it matters

- Spotify ended Q3 2025 with **281 million** Premium subscribers and **713 million** monthly active users (Music Business Worldwide, 2025). Growth now depends as much on keeping subscribers as on adding them.
- Students are the cheapest subscribers to acquire and, in the long run, among the most valuable: they arrive young and could stay for decades.
- The competition is waiting at exactly this moment. A graduate reviewing subscriptions is one bundle offer away from switching to Apple Music through Apple One, or to YouTube Music through YouTube Premium.

## The opportunity

**Turn graduation from a price shock into a milestone.** If Spotify helps graduates choose the right plan before the student discount ends, more of them stay paying, and more of them stay on Spotify at all.

## Goal

> Increase the share of Premium Student subscribers who are **on any paid Spotify plan 90 days after their student eligibility ends**.

This is the North Star metric for the concept. It counts Individual, Duo and Family plans equally, because a graduate who joins a friend's Duo plan is still a paying Spotify listener.

## What this concept is and isn't

- **It is** a growth plan: a funnel model, prioritised experiments and the metrics to judge them.
- **It isn't** based on Spotify's internal data. Every funnel number is an illustrative assumption, labelled as such, so the logic can be checked and the numbers replaced with real ones.
